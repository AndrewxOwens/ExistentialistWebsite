# In Search of Meaning

A philosophy final project exploring how to live a meaningful life when the world offers no clear answers.

---

## About

This website examines the question of meaning through the perspectives of four existentialist and existentialist-adjacent philosophers: **Sartre**, **Camus**, **Heidegger**, and **Kierkegaard**. Each thinker approached the absence of inherent meaning differently, and each offers a distinct way of living inside that uncertainty.

The site also includes a **reflection feature**: a set of questions rooted in everyday experience that bridge personal doubts to philosophical ideas, helping users discover which philosophical traditions speak to their own way of thinking.

---

## Pages & Features

- **Landing page** — introduces the central question and the four philosophers
- **Philosopher sections** — each philosopher's key ideas, core concepts, and connection to the question of meaning
- **Reflection quiz** — 5 questions connecting everyday experience to philosophical thought
